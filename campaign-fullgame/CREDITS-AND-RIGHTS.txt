PROJECT AUDIO, FONTS AND ART

Character voices, the procedural group audience voice, selected-date Frankie pulse atoms and the celebration chime are generated from the project’s original local synthesis code. They are abstract voices, not recorded human performances or third-party speech-service output. The natural laughter credits above also cover the reused final tag laugh.

The block fonts, cursor, GM instrument mapping and diagnostic MIDI bed/stinger/credit cue trace to original project code. Opening/credits sound resource 3 is an original provisional cue, not the complete revised score.

The retained living-room music cue, sound resource 102 (MUS02_HOUSE_LIVING), is a source arrangement of user-supplied MIDI. The user asserted ownership of the supplied Suno MIDI sources and authorized adaptation. This audit does not independently certify those ownership claims. No recorded score or SoundFont is included in the player data.

Artwork includes original generated raster work, original code-native elements and retained historical project assets. P03 views 221–224, 300 and 302 have exact file continuity but incomplete individual original-creation records. Other recovered historical candidate artwork has matching delivered-preview evidence. rights/ART-PROVENANCE.json and rights/PROVENANCE.md identify those qualifications. New generated reconstruction is not described as recovered historical artwork.
