# Rights and licensing

Original project code, scripts, generated artwork and procedural abstract voices retain their existing rights. No new blanket open-source or redistribution license for the game or user-provided music has been selected by this snapshot. Public repository visibility, if chosen, does not change that statement.

Third-party source, compiler tooling and asset material retain their own licenses and notices. This player package contains game data, credits and asset-rights notices; it does not include the compiler or its source. The separate source distribution includes the pinned compiler, its build-verified minimal corresponding source and required notices under `source/toolchain/source`. In that separate distribution, review the license files at `source/toolchain/source/lucasartsifier` and within that source tree for their exact terms.

ScummVM, FluidSynth and FluidR3 are separate runtime dependencies; their runtime/SoundFont binaries are not bundled. Natural audience and Foley recordings retain their individually identified CC0, CC BY 4.0 or CC BY 3.0 terms and required credits in `CREDITS.txt` and the corresponding manifests in the separate source distribution. New performance derivatives do not change the source license. This candidate does not assert that every historical source was individually auditioned or newly user-approved.

For the scope and historical context of the included rights records, see `RIGHTS-CONTEXT.md`.
