# Rights and licensing

Original project code, scripts, generated artwork and procedural abstract voices retain their existing rights. No new blanket open-source or redistribution license for the game or user-provided music has been selected by this snapshot. Public repository visibility, if chosen, does not change that statement.

Third-party source, compiler tooling and asset material retain their own included licenses and notices. The bundled pinned compiler is accompanied by its build-verified minimal corresponding source and required notices under `source/toolchain/source`. Review the license files at `source/toolchain/source/lucasartsifier` and within that source tree for their exact terms.

ScummVM, FluidSynth and FluidR3 are separate runtime dependencies; their runtime/SoundFont binaries are not bundled. Natural audience and Foley recordings retain their individually identified CC0, CC BY 4.0 or CC BY 3.0 terms and required credits in `CREDITS-AND-RIGHTS.txt` and the corresponding source manifests. New performance derivatives do not change the source license. This candidate does not assert that every historical source was individually auditioned or newly user-approved.
